About Daniel Villamizar

Daniel Villamizar is a Senior SAP Consultant and engineering professional based in Montreal. He has more than 15 years of experience delivering SAP solutions and more than a decade of additional experience in maintenance, reliability, asset management and process optimization.

His background combines SAP functional consulting with real industrial knowledge, which helps bridge the gap between system design and business operations. Through VillaConsultants, Daniel supports organizations looking for a practical consultant who understands both enterprise systems and operational realities on the ground.
